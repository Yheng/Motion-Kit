{{TACHI-07}}. Armoured combat exoskeleton.

Nine metres of articulated plate over a hydraulic frame. Built to hold ground in dense cities, and to be repaired in one.

A machine you can actually keep running

  • Plate, not skin

    Every panel is a serviceable part. Armour comes off in sections with hand tools, so damage is a replacement rather than a recovery.

  • One pilot

    A single seat, direct linkage, and no crew to coordinate. The machine does what the person in it decided to do.

  • Built for streets

    Sized and geared for held ground between buildings, not open country. It walks where the people it protects already are.

Systems

  1. Frame

    A load-bearing internal skeleton carries the weight; the plate carries nothing but itself. Damage to the outside does not compromise the structure.

  2. Articulation

    Exposed hydraulic actuators at knee and elbow. Deliberately outside the armour line, where they can be inspected and swapped without a lift.

  3. Power

    {{Nine-hour}} continuous operation at patrol load, with a hot-swap cell behind the left shoulder guard.
